Buying bulk

Why bulk beats bags

A cubic yard of mulch covers about 100 square feet at three inches deep, and it takes roughly thirteen and a half store bags to equal it. Once the beds get measured honestly, most yards need several yards, and the math tips hard toward bulk: less plastic, less loading, less money per square foot.

Timing helps too. Mulch wants to go down after spring soil warms; topsoil and compost work spring or fall; stone goes in any month the ground isn't frozen. And if we're doing the install as well, ask on the same call: the landscaping crew and the delivery truck come from the same yard.

Good questions

Deliveries, asked and answered

How many yards of mulch do I need?

A cubic yard covers about 100 square feet at three inches deep. Measure the length and width of each bed, punch it into the calculator above, and we round up to the half yard so you don't run short on the last corner.

Do I need to be home for delivery?

No. Mark an X on the driveway with chalk, tape or a cone, and the driver drops the load on the X. We confirm the price and the day by phone before the truck rolls.

What's the difference between clean and modified stone?

Clean stone is washed and uniform, so it drains: French drains, pipe bedding, dry wells. Modified has fines mixed in so it compacts tight: driveways, patio bases, anything that needs to lock up. Tell us what you're building and we'll put the right one on the truck.

When will my order arrive?

We confirm the price and the day when you order, usually within one business day of your request. Spring moves fast; ordering ahead of the season holds your spot.
