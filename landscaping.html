Growing in Montgomery and Bucks

What our yards deal with

Lawns around Horsham grow on heavy clay soil that packs down hard by late summer. That's why fall aeration and overseeding do more for a thick spring lawn than any bag of fertilizer: the cores open the clay, the seed lands in real soil, and cool-season grasses take hold before frost.

Beds want the opposite rhythm. A clean spring edge and two to three inches of fresh mulch hold moisture through July, and a proper fall cleanup keeps matted leaves from smothering turf over winter. The mulch we spread is the same triple ground brown we sell by the yard on our delivery page: one crew, one supplier, no middleman.

Good questions

Landscaping, asked and answered

How much does landscaping cost in Montgomery County?

It depends on the yard, which is why every number starts with a free walk-through. Weekly mowing, one-time cleanups and full bed renovations are each priced on their own terms, measured on site and quoted fair, in writing. No padding, no surprises. Request yours here.

When should I book a spring or fall cleanup?

Spring cleanups run March into May; fall cleanups start when the leaves do, October into December. The calendar fills a few weeks ahead in both seasons, so the best time to call is before the season turns.

Do you offer weekly mowing service?

Yes. Weekly cuts with edging and trimming through the growing season, on a route, the same crew all year. Not a different truck every visit.

Do I need to be home when the crew comes?

No. Most maintenance visits happen while you are at work. Gates get closed, debris gets hauled, and if something needs your eyes, Gary calls.
